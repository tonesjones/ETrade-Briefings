# Briefing history retention

Daily Etrade tasks provide an audit trail of the generated prompt, completed
analysis, and the owner’s decision. Creating one task per market day is useful,
but the active project should not grow without a retention rule.

Before archiving older daily tasks, determine what historical depth materially
improves decisions. Evaluate this using the durable local records, not tab count:

- portfolio and lot snapshots for tax and execution reconciliation;
- captured proposal records for recommendation continuity and trigger review;
- weekly/monthly summaries for allocation drift, realized tax events, and thesis changes.

Candidate policy to validate:

- keep the most recent 20 trading-day tasks active for day-to-day continuity;
- retain older dated tasks as archived history, searchable if needed;
- keep local snapshots and captured decision records for at least one tax year,
  subject to later review of storage and practical value;
- preserve any task containing an approved, executed, or materially changed
  proposal in the local decision record before archival.

Decide the useful lookback after enough captured records exist to compare
recommendation changes with subsequent evidence and outcomes. Do not delete
local records solely to reduce sidebar clutter.
